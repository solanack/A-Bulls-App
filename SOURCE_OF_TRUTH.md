# A Bulls App — source of truth

The Field is the home screen.

- App opens on the living particle Field. Not the head.
- Each particle is a living point: wallet, tx, token, NFT, or program.
- Touch a particle: it lights up, same-kind particles lean in, a line appears under the chin. No new screen.
- ASK is what makes the Field swarm into the Grey head.
- After the swarm the head stays alive.
- Empty tap clears focus. Explore returns to Field form.
