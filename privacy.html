Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

A Bulls App ("the App," "we," "us") is published by DingDong Vibes. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and what it never does. The App is available at abullsapp.com and as an installable Android app.

The short version: The App never asks you to connect a wallet, sign a message, approve a transaction, or share a private key or seed phrase. Solana address lookups are read-only. Your device stores your local profile and media. This launch release has no in-app purchases or payment checkout.

1. Information you provide directly

  • Google sign-in (optional): If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's identity token, which we verify against Google's own servers. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Public Solana wallet address (optional): If you paste a public wallet address to view holdings or activity, we use that address only to query public blockchain data on your behalf. We never ask for or accept a private key, seed phrase, or wallet signature of any kind.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Game scores, run history, and settings stored locally in your browser/app storage.
  • Any profile images, avatars, or media you upload — these remain on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

3. Ranked game data stored on our servers

To provide the Ranked leaderboard, our Cloudflare-hosted backend stores:

  • Your chosen public display alias, submitted score, run time, kills, boss count, run mode, and submission timestamp.
  • A pseudonymous account identifier. For Google users this is a one-way hash derived from the verified Google account identifier; we do not store the raw Google subject in these game tables. Signed-out users receive a random, signed player session stored on their device.

Leaderboard aliases and scores are public. The other records are used to operate the features shown in the App. Cloudflare may also process request metadata such as IP address for delivery, abuse prevention, and operational logs under its own policies.

4. Payment data

This launch release does not sell Bullion, digital goods, subscriptions, cryptocurrency, NFTs, or any other product. It does not open Stripe Checkout or Google Play Billing and does not collect payment or purchase-history data. If purchases are added in a later version, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before that feature goes live.

5. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect private keys, seed phrases, or wallet signatures.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or contacts.
  • We do not sell personal information to third parties.

6. Third-party services we rely on

  • Google Identity Services — for optional sign-in.
  • Helius — a Solana blockchain data provider we use server-side to read public on-chain and NFT data. Your API requests to us are proxied through our own server; Helius never receives your identity, only the public address you asked us to look up.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the App and its backend infrastructure.
  • Magic Eden — we link out to Magic Eden listings for NFTs; we do not control their site or its data practices.
  • YouTube / Twitch — embedded video/stream players, subject to their own respective privacy policies when in use.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the email below and we will remove it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data (scores, settings, uploaded media, and the signed player session) can be cleared at any time using the App's "Reset" option or your browser/app storage controls. Clearing local data does not automatically delete server leaderboard records. Google sign-in sessions can be revoked from your Google account settings. To request deletion of server-side data associated with a signed-in or anonymous player session, contact us below from the affected device where possible; records required for security or legal obligations may be retained as permitted by law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date at the top of this page.
