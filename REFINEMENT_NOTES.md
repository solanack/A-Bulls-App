# A Bulls App — Template-Alien Correction

> Historical organism-refinement snapshot. It documents why the current Grey looks and behaves the way it does, but deployment URLs and product-scope statements here are not the active release contract. Use `AGENTS.md`, `UNIVERSE_VISION.md`, `REVIEW_2026-09-08.md`, `FINAL_RELEASE.md`, and `CLOUDFLARE_DEPLOYMENT.md` for the current release.

This pass fixed the QUERY organism itself. The earlier build imported the supplied alien template only to check orientation, while a procedural head profile still generated the visible face. That procedural fallback was removed.

## Corrected organism

- `alien-head-template.mjs` is the geometry authority for the visible skin, the depth core, and the feature layout.
- The 18,000 template samples and their semantic roles drive the cranium, silhouette, eyes, nose, and mouth.
- Eye, nostril, and mouth positions are derived from template-role bounds instead of hand-placed constants.
- Nested template shells create opaque near-black depth without replacing the supplied silhouette.
- Eye surfaces are recessed and restrained; the mouth is a narrow living slit instead of a rectangular block.
- The exposed eye inserts are concealed by a curved, fitted Solana-gradient Pit Viper visor with a black brow frame, nose bridge, and Solana-color temple marks.
- The near-black depth core uses circular shader points, eliminating the square/block artifacts visible on the forehead in earlier builds.
- The camera frames the template bounds per viewport, including portrait/mobile screens.
- Field points use round point sprites, removing visible square artifacts.
- Cellular motion, breathing, and speech deformation remain subtle so the anatomy stays stable.

## Voice and mobile behavior

- The Web Audio context is unlocked during the user gesture and reused across queries.
- Speech is slower and lower-pitched, with a restrained subharmonic carrier.
- Random mouth pulses were removed; speech deformation follows deterministic organism state.
- The device budgets and reduced-motion/save-data paths remain in place.

## Architecture preserved by this historical pass

- The normal Particle Field remained the entry surface.
- QUERY continued to accept public wallet, transaction, mint, NFT, and program identifiers.
- The read-only Intelligence Worker boundary was preserved.
- No wallet connection, commerce, or retired shooter surface was added.

The Worker hostname written in older copies of these notes was a point-in-time deployment detail and is intentionally not repeated as an active endpoint. The current deployment contract resolves the production Worker/service binding from repository configuration.
