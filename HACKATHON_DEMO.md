# A Bulls App — judge demo runbook

Target: 90–120 seconds. Show production, not mockups.

## Story
A Bulls App turns public blockchain activity into an explorable evidence universe: study the trader, replay the trade, verify the story. Afterbell demonstrates the same evidence-first interaction model applied to tokenized equities without adding execution or signing.

## Shot order
1. **0:00–0:08 — Field**: open `abullsapp.com`. One sentence: “A Bulls App turns blockchain history into an explorable universe.”
2. **0:08–0:28 — Trader STAR**: enter Fomo, select a real mapped trader STAR, reveal bounded token PLANETS and retained trade COMETS. Call out provider-reported versus chain-observed evidence.
3. **0:28–0:50 — Replay**: choose a real wallet × mint trade, open Replay, show real indexed events/candles and receipt linkage. Say: “No invented cost basis. No fabricated candles.”
4. **0:50–1:02 — Verify**: open Evidence/VERIFY for the selected thread/Cut. Show that the story resolves back to signatures and the exact evidence window.
5. **1:02–1:25 — Afterbell**: open `/afterbell/`, strike the bell, show xStock PLANETS populated from live venue-reported Solana data, select NVDAx or MSFTx, then show House Book and evidence tape. Say: “The same evidence grammar expands beyond memecoins without turning the product into a broker.”
6. **1:25–1:35 — Close**: return to the Field. End on: “Study the trader. Replay the trade. Verify the story.”

## Narration
“A Bulls App is an evidence-native Solana research operating system rendered as a universe. Instead of another trading terminal, we let you enter a real public wallet, see what it actually traded, descend into a specific token and matched round, replay the activity against indexed market data, and inspect the receipts behind the story. Provider context stays labeled. Missing evidence stays missing. Nothing is fabricated. The same architecture can extend to new onchain markets: Afterbell turns tokenized equities into a live research floor, using venue-reported Solana data while keeping the product read-only. A Bulls App makes blockchain history explorable, replayable, and verifiable. Study the trader. Replay the trade. Verify the story.”

## Recording rules
- Record the production domain only after the release SHA is verified live.
- Use a real Fomo trader with mapped wallet and retained trades.
- Do not show empty/loading states unless explaining coverage honestly.
- Keep taps deliberate; no debugging panels, dashboards, terminals, or GitHub in the final cut.
- Capture at 1080p/60 when possible; final export 1080p H.264, 30 or 60 fps.
- Keep source labels visible long enough for judges to read them.
- No financial-performance promises, recommendations, or execution demo.
