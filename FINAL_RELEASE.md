# Living Universe completion record

The PDF in `docs/a-bulls-app-universe-brief.pdf` is the governing product specification.

## Completed

- Galaxy Zero preserved as the reference particle field and generalized through immutable launch-origin state.
- pump.fun populated through the same universe, event, provenance, route, and candle tables.
- Replay, Evidence, Compare, What-If, Sequences, Ghost, and Trickster are functional read-only interfaces.
- Real indexed OHLC is rendered as candlesticks; missing market data remains visibly empty.
- Trickster validates every claim against evidence receipts before optional frozen sharing.
- Education is narrated and noncompetitive.
- One D1 database-backed Helius reservation breaker protects both QUERY and background history indexing.
- Passive render, camera, particle, Replay scrub, and Evidence display actions do not call Helius.
- Legacy competitive runtime, routes, database binding, reconstruction scripts, and assets are removed from the release.
- Wallet connection, custody, signing, minting, transaction submission, and commerce are absent.

## Deployment-owned configuration

Real coverage still depends on the existing account's `INTELLIGENCE_DB`, `HELIUS_API_KEY`, pump webhook, and ingestion health. The application never invents credentials, candles, provenance, or claims when those sources are empty.

The future public SDK/platform layer described in the brief remains intentionally outside this release.
