# A Bulls App — Living Universe Direction

> `MASTER_PLAN.md` is the highest-level product contract. This file is the compact universe/Field engineering contract and must be interpreted in that context.

## Product definition
A Bulls App is an evidence-native Solana research operating system rendered as an explorable universe. The Field is the discovery/spatial layer; Intelligence, Index, Trickster, Evidence, Replay, Ghost, and My Sky are alternate interfaces onto the same evidence graph.

## Non-negotiable boundaries
- Research-first and non-custodial: no swaps, transaction signing, copy trading, custody, approvals, token launch, liquidity execution, or automated trade routing.
- The default web product remains usable without wallet connection. The only approved future wallet-adapter exception is the narrow mobile `Bind My Star` public-address authorization described in `MASTER_PLAN.md`; it must not sign or execute anything.
- Retired shooter/LIFE/Bull Invaders surfaces must not return.
- Extend the current particle Field, Grey organism, Replay, Evidence, Trickster/Create, Index/data contracts, and Intelligence Worker; do not create a second product.
- Grey speaks observed/sourced facts and explicit coverage. Trickster speaks interpretation/story. Provider-reported context remains provider-reported.
- Decorative fabric is DUST only and never promoted into evidence.

## Public universe topology
Galaxy Zero exposes exactly three primary destinations:
- **Fomo** — trader research lens. Up to 50 cached Fomo all-time trader identities become public-wallet STARS when a usable public wallet is supplied.
- **pump.fun** — launch-origin galaxy of token PLANETS.
- **PonsFamily** — public name for internal galaxy id `pons`; qualifying verified PONS-origin token PLANETS are self-populated and ranked by fresh reported 24h volume.

`solana-core` remains an internal provenance/compatibility namespace. It is not a public Galaxy Zero destination. Generic Solana wallets, tokens, Replay, Evidence, and Index records remain queryable.

A research lens never rewrites launch origin.

## Canonical research descent
The Field should perform one continuous narrowing path:

**Universe → Galaxy → Planet → Holder Sky → Star → Holdings → Matched Rounds → Chosen Trade → Tools → Trickster Cut**

A planet entry should dolly inward rather than cut to a dashboard. The token remains low/central while retained wallet STARS populate the sky above. Missing holder evidence leaves the sky empty and Grey states the limitation.

Selecting a STAR keeps the user inside the same research system and reveals observed/provider-reported position PLANETS with explicit source labels. Selecting a matched round creates/updates the shared Research Thread so Replay, Evidence, Compare, What-If, Sequences, Ghost, Intelligence, and Trickster inherit `wallet + mint + time window + receipts` automatically.

## Research Thread
The Research Thread is the persistent context backbone for deep research. It should carry the active galaxy/lens, immutable launch origin, mint, wallet, selected matched round, timestamps, signatures, Replay state, Evidence IDs, Ghost/Sequence/Compare references, thesis/narration references, watch state, and Cut manifests as applicable.

Tools should consume the active thread instead of asking the user to re-enter known context.

## Token planet entry
A token PLANET is navigable. Entering it keeps the token central while retained public wallets become STARS around it. Star size/behavior can use only explicitly documented observed relationship measures. Missing holder/wallet evidence produces an honest empty sky. Planet entry and rendering must not trigger passive provider fan-out. Indexed current trades may appear as COMETS and observed liquidity as an ASTEROID BELT.

## Fomo Galaxy and trader systems
The Fomo all-time top 50 is a discovery layer sourced from the approved independent feed and cached in D1. Page views never replay a logged-in Fomo session.

Fomo rank, PnL, profile, follower, volume, and reported top-token fields are labeled **Fomo-reported / as reported**. They are not A Bulls App proof of profit, identity, skill, strategy, or wallet control.

Entering a Fomo trader STAR creates a bounded research system:
- selected trader STAR at the center;
- up to 10 token PLANETS mapped from provider-reported positions and/or retained A Bulls App wallet-token observations;
- explicit source label per position;
- up to 3 latest retained trade COMETS;
- direct descent into the trader's history on a selected token;
- automatic Research Thread context inheritance for Replay/Evidence/Compare/Sequences/Ghost/Trickster.

No copy-trade action exists.

## PonsFamily
PonsFamily keeps internal id `pons` to preserve D1 identity and factory-origin history. Membership is automatic and evidence-gated:
- verified PONS V1/V2 launch-origin receipt required;
- fresh market cap **above $75,000**;
- fresh holder count **above 750**;
- positive/fresh reported 24-hour volume required;
- ranking primarily by **24-hour volume descending**;
- maximum 50 qualifying planets;
- FDV never substitutes for market cap;
- missing holder/market evidence does not qualify.

The old pinned $PONS teaching-token fallback is retired. Empty qualifying membership is preferable to fabricated planets.

## Matched rounds
The wallet×mint research view should surface deterministic trade rounds:
- **matched closed** — retained evidence supports an in-window acquisition basis and matched exit;
- **open** — observed inventory remains open, no realized result;
- **unmatched sell** — excluded from realized accounting when acquisition basis is unknown.

Never invent cost basis or label these as alpha, smart money, or trader skill.

## Replay
Replay is the timeline primitive. It must use indexed OHLC/candles when available, support deterministic seeking/playback, show trade markers and receipt jumps, and persist/share the exact Research Thread window.

If OHLC is unavailable, the app may show the event timeline but must not draw a fabricated price path.

## Ghost
Ghost is evidence-backed historical similarity, not prediction. It may compare the active Research Thread with a prior sequence by the same wallet, another indexed wallet, or the same token under a comparable deterministic regime. Matching criteria, time windows, coverage, and receipts must remain inspectable.

## Trickster / media
Trickster is the director/interpretation layer. Grey remains the observation layer.

A Trickster Cut should be built from the selected Research Thread and indexed chart/event tape. Prefer deterministic browser media generation via Mediabunny/WebCodecs when capability allows, with graceful fallback. Every durable Cut must have a manifest and a VERIFY/Replay path that reopens the exact evidence state.

## Index
The Index is a first-class archive alongside the Field. Traders, tokens, trades, matched rounds, Research Threads, Replay windows, Evidence bundles, Cuts, theses/resolutions, Ghosts, Sequences, and useful comparisons should be searchable and cross-linked by stable IDs/timestamps.

Universe and Index must deep-link both directions. Conventional Study Trader and Study Token pages are approved and should complement—not replace—the Field.

## My Sky / watch
My Sky may save token PLANETS, public-wallet STARS, Research Threads, Cuts, and useful evidence objects. Alerts are observational and should deep-link to the exact Universe/Index/thread context.

## Grey voice
Grey uses server-side ElevenLabs synthesis when configured, with a dedicated **natural human, calm, clear, premium** voice profile and browser speech fallback. The ElevenLabs API key stays server-side. Do not restore metallic/demon/low-pitch alien processing.

## Indexing and evidence coverage
Indexing is a core product capability. Public navigation stays cache/D1-first, provider calls stay bounded/scheduled, and missing coverage remains explicit.

Long-term approved ingestion direction is a small upstream Solana indexing service using technologies such as **Carbon + Yellowstone gRPC** for live stream, gap detection, backfill through the same normalization pipeline, and independent coverage verification. This should feed the existing A Bulls App product contracts rather than replace the Worker/D1 application layer.

## Performance and local intelligence
Preserve mobile, reduced-motion, save-data, and the known-good Seeker WebGL lifecycle. Do not increase Field particle budgets merely to support new research objects.

Approved future supporting technologies:
- **Mediabunny/WebCodecs** for deterministic Cut export;
- **DuckDB-Wasm** for lazy-loaded local Evidence Pack analytics;
- **Graphology** for wallet↔token relationship analysis while keeping the current Field renderer;
- **Transformers.js** only for bounded evidence retrieval/search, never for inventing blockchain facts;
- selective WebGPU compute behind capability detection, not a risky core Field rewrite.
